\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Supplementary material}\\[8pt]
{\large Learning to Fix Retiming and Reordering Decisions in Railway Timetable Rescheduling}
\end{center}
This document accompanies the manuscript and its reproducibility repository.
Sections S1 and S4 together define all 267 candidate catalog items: S1 covers the 191 items with at least one estimable, nonconstant numerical form, while S4 records the remaining items and why no association is reported.
Section S3 preserves all 6,402 estimable association responses without a significance filter, together with prediction-input selection frequencies.
Section S2 gives the complete computation-time accounting. Section S5 gives the prediction architectures and key parameter settings.
The archived machine-readable results accompany this document; unavailable or constant indicators are not assigned fabricated correlations.
The study uses 75 training and test scenarios and 36 transfer scenarios on one railway network and one planned timetable.
