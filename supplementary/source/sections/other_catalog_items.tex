Together with Section~\ref{app:indicators}, this section completes the 267-item candidate catalog. The 76 items below have no reported association: 39 have only constant forms where defined, 26 cannot be calculated with the available model and data, and 11 describe operating-state, strategy, or experiment context but are absent from the descriptive-association inventory. The complete estimable results for the other 191 items are retained in Section~\ref{app:association-results}.

An unavailable or constant association is not a zero correlation. Missing or inapplicable values are not replaced by zero. The availability classification below follows the recorded numerical inventory and model coverage, not the preliminary catalog flags. Definitions retain the intended measurement, while references to the present design describe the current experiment. For context items, the absence of an estimated association does not imply that the quantity is irrelevant to model construction.
