Enclosing process times are not added to the components they already contain. The full-reference and chained settings for Strategy-3 include the costs of obtaining full and restricted Strategy-2 timetables, respectively; FULL Strategy-3 starts from Strategy-1 and does not require Strategy-2. \paragraph{Offline preparation.}
The five-fold training runs, including internal model selection and calibration, require 1,061.09 seconds for STAT and 2,249.27 seconds for ML across the two strategy expansions. Fitting the final models to all training and test scenarios requires another 272.43 seconds for STAT and 592.44 seconds for ML; these models are then frozen for transfer. These training times are excluded from complete online time.
